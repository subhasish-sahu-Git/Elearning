## 2026-03-26 - [Accessibility Gaps in Bootstrap Template Elements]
**Learning:** Common interactive elements in this Bootstrap-based template, such as the `.navbar-toggler` and `.back-to-top` button, consistently lacked ARIA labels for screen readers. Additionally, form labels in the contact page were incorrectly associated with their inputs due to ID/for mismatches.
**Action:** When working with similar templates, proactively audit the navbar togglers, scroll-to-top buttons, and form-floating labels for proper ARIA attributes and ID associations.
